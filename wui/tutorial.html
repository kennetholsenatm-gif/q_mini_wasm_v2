🧮 Understanding Ternary Computing

Unlike traditional binary computing that uses 0 and 1, ternary computing operates with three states: -1, 0, and +1. This provides more efficient representation of quantum information and enables better energy efficiency.

Key Benefits of Ternary Computing:

  • Higher information density per trit
  • Natural representation for quantum states
  • Reduced circuit complexity
  • Improved energy efficiency

⚛️ Quantum Operations with Stabilizer Tableaus

q_mini_wasm_v2 uses stabilizer tableaus to represent quantum states efficiently. This approach, based on the Gottesman-Knill theorem, allows for efficient classical simulation of quantum operations.

Supported Quantum Gates:

  • Hadamard (H): Creates superposition states
  • Phase (S): Adds quantum phase
  • CSUM (Controlled-SUM): Entangles qutrits

🌐 QGNN Graph-Native Architecture

The Quantum Graph Neural Network (QGNN) architecture replaces traditional O(N²) arrays with O(E) sparse graphs, providing significant performance and energy improvements.

Key Features:

  • Linear scaling with graph size
  • Quantum message passing
  • Graph attention mechanisms
  • Seamless migration from array-based systems
