# Public Domain/Open Text Corpora for Training

## FREE AND OPEN RESOURCES:

1. PROJECT GUTENBERG (40,000+ public domain books)
   - URL: https://www.gutenberg.org/
   - License: Public Domain (US)
   - Format: Plain text, HTML, EPUB

2. WIKIPEDIA DUMP (English, 20GB+ text)
   - URL: https://dumps.wikimedia.org/enwiki/
   - License: CC BY-SA 3.0
   - Format: XML/Wiki markup

3. COMMON CRAWL (web-scale corpus, petabytes)
   - URL: https://commoncrawl.org/
   - License: Various (filtered for open content)
   - Format: WARC, WET (extracted text)

4. OPENWEBTEXT (40GB web text, GPT-2 training data)
   - URL: https://github.com/jcpeterson/openwebtext
   - License: Public domain / open content
   - Format: JSONL

5. BOOK CORPUS (11,000 unpublished books - not freely available)
   Alternative: GUTENBERG + SMASHWORDS

6. BNC (British National Corpus) - Academic license required
   Alternative: OPENSUBTITLES

7. COCA (Corpus of Contemporary American) - Academic license
   Alternative: iWeb Corpus samples
