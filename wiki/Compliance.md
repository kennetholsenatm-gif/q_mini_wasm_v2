# Legal and Compliance

## Overview

Q-Mini-WASM implements comprehensive legal and compliance frameworks to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements, industry standards, and legal obligations. Our legal and compliance approach combines multiple compliance standards, legal documentation, and regulatory adherence to provide a legally sound quantum computing platform.

- **HIPAA Compliance**: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act compliance
- **PCI DSS Compliance**: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard compliance
- **SOX Compliance**: Sarbanes-Oxley Act compliance

- **ISO 27001**: Information Security Management System certification
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Service Organization Control compliance
- **FedRAMP**: Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program compliance
- **NIST SP 800-53**: National Institute of Standards and Technology security controls
